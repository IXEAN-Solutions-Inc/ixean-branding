Presentation intros, website interactions and loaders for the IXEAN mark. Each sequence is built from the master vector paths, and every one resolves on the exact brand lockup. The mark is read as a circuit: four rotor nodes, four traces and a single bus, so motion travels the way a signal would.

IXN-P1 · Ignition4.5 s

Intro · 16:9 · 4.5 s + idle

Ignition

Nodes power up, traces etch toward the bus and the four signals meet in a bloom. The wordmark scans in behind a cyan edge and the tagline decodes. A telemetry pulse then runs the circuit every four seconds.

IXN-P2 · Formation3.6 s

Intro · vertical lockup · 3.6 s

Formation

Four nodes fly in from the frame corners like a quad converging on station, rotors spinning down as each one lands. The traces connect them, then the wordmark and tagline settle underneath.

IXN-P3 · Dispersal3.4 s

Outro · end card · 3.4 s

Dispersal

The lockup holds, then the nodes lift off toward the corners and leave cyan flight trails. The traces cool and fade, and the frame returns to obsidian.
